Privacy Policy

8 Ball Pool

Effective date: November 1, 2026
Last updated: November 1, 2026
App: 8 Ball Pool (package com.eightball.pool)
Developer: Chawla Labs

1. Overview

This privacy policy describes how the Android app 8 Ball Pool (“the App”) handles information. The App is a local pool game: solo against the computer, or pass-and-play with a friend on the same device. It does not require an internet connection, does not create user accounts, and does not send your data to the developer or to any third-party service.

2. Information we collect

The App stores a small set of settings only on your device, using Android DataStore preferences:

  • Table sounds on or off
  • Vibration on or off
  • Aim line on or off

For a pass-and-play match, display names you type (up to 16 characters) are used to label the players during that match. They stay in the current session on the device. They are not saved as an account and are not written to a server.

The current rack — ball positions, whose turn it is, groups, and the called pocket — is held in memory so the game can run. It is discarded when you leave the match.

The App does not collect personal identifiers such as email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising IDs. It does not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs. Table sounds are bundled in the App and play on the device only.

3. How information is used

On-device data is used solely to:

  • Remember your sound, vibration, and aim-line preferences
  • Show player names during a pass-and-play match
  • Run the current rack on your device

This information is not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share your App data with third parties. Because the App does not transmit settings, names, or gameplay off the device, there is no server-side copy held by the developer.

Google Play and the Android operating system may process limited technical information (for example, install and update metadata) under their own privacy policies when you download or update the App from Google Play. That processing is controlled by Google, not by this App.

5. Permissions

The App requests only:

  • Vibrate — optional haptic feedback on shots and results. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

The App does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions.

6. Data retention and deletion

Settings remain on your device until you change them or clear the App’s data. Uninstalling the App removes local App data from that device, subject to any device backup you have enabled. A match in progress is not kept after you leave it.

Android backup is enabled and may include the App’s on-device preferences (sound, vibration, and aim line). Those backups are governed by the relevant Google or device-maker privacy policy.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a local pool game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not require an account or online features. If you believe a child has provided personal information through some other channel related to this App, contact us using the details below and we will address the request.

8. Security

Because data stays on your device, security depends on your device’s own protections (screen lock, OS updates, and who has physical access). We do not operate a backend that stores your App data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy if the App’s data practices change (for example, if online features are added later). The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised, and the new policy will be posted at this URL.
