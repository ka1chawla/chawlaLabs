Privacy Policy

Toddler Learning games

Effective date: October 30, 2026
Last updated: October 30, 2026
App: Toddler Learning games (on-device name Toddler Games, package com.toddler.games)
Developer: Chawla Labs

1. Overview

This privacy policy describes how the Android app Toddler Learning games (“the App”) handles information. The App is a set of offline learning games for young children: Color Match, Shape Match, Number Order, Letter Order, Big & Small Letters, and Letters & Numbers. It does not require an internet connection, does not create user accounts, and does not send your data to the developer or to any third-party service.

2. Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information and does not write gameplay or settings to storage on your device. There is no database, no preferences file, and no account.

While a level is open, the current round — which items are on screen and how many correct taps remain — is held in memory only so that level can run. That session state is discarded when you leave the level or close the App. Levels are always available from 1 to 10; the App does not save progress, scores, names, or settings.

Sound effects (correct, wrong, win, and tap) are bundled inside the App and play on the device only.

The App does not collect personal identifiers such as email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising IDs. It does not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.

3. How information is used

In-memory session state is used solely to run the level you are playing. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing, and it is not transmitted off the device.

4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share App data with third parties. Because the App does not transmit gameplay or any other data off the device, there is no server-side copy held by the developer.

Google Play and the Android operating system may process limited technical information (for example, install and update metadata) under their own privacy policies when you download or update the App from Google Play. That processing is controlled by Google, not by this App.

5. Permissions

The App does not request any permissions. It does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or vibrate.

6. Data retention and deletion

The App does not keep an account or a stored gameplay record, so there is no profile or score history to delete. Uninstalling the App removes it from that device.

Android backup is allowed by the App manifest. Because the App does not write personal or gameplay data to storage, there is no personal information from the App for a device backup to include. If Android Backup / device transfer is enabled, any platform backup is governed by the relevant Google or device-maker privacy policy.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is made for toddlers and young children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else. It has no accounts, chat, social features, advertising, or analytics, and it does not require an internet connection.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through some other channel related to this App, contact us using the details below and we will address the request.

8. Security

The App does not transmit or store personal data. We do not operate a backend that stores App data. Any protection of the device itself (screen lock, OS updates, and who has physical access) is controlled by you and by the device maker.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy if the App’s data practices change (for example, if online features are added later). The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised, and the new policy will be posted at this URL.
