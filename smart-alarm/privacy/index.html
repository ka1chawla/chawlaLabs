Privacy Policy

Smart Alarm

Effective date: October 31, 2026
Last updated: October 31, 2026
App: Smart Alarm (package com.smartalarm.app)
Developer: Chawla Labs

1. Overview

This privacy policy describes how the Android app Smart Alarm (“the App”) handles information. The App is a local alarm clock. You set a time and a wake-up mission, and the alarm rings on your device until that mission is finished. It does not require an internet connection, does not create user accounts, and does not send your data to the developer or to any third-party service.

2. Information we collect

The App stores information only on your device so alarms can ring when you expect them to. There is no account and no server copy held by the developer.

Alarms are saved in a local database on the device. Each alarm may include:

  • The time, a label you type, and whether the alarm is on
  • Vibration, snooze length, and which days it repeats
  • The wake-up mission and its targets (steps, taps, or math difficulty)
  • The ringtone or voice clip you chose, stored as a name and a local reference

A ringtone stays where the system already keeps it. The App stores a reference so it can play that sound. A voice clip you pick is copied into the App’s private storage on the device so the alarm can play it later. Neither file is uploaded.

Settings are saved in on-device preferences: the default mission for new alarms, alarm volume, and whether volume ramps up gradually.

During Walk it off, step counts are read from the phone’s step sensor only while that mission is on screen. During Shake to stop, motion is read from the accelerometer only while that mission is on screen. Those readings are not saved after the mission ends.

The App does not collect personal identifiers such as email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising IDs. It does not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.

3. How information is used

On-device data is used solely to:

  • Schedule and ring alarms, including after a reboot or a time change
  • Show the alarm on the lock screen and play the sound you chose
  • Run the wake-up mission until you dismiss or snooze
  • Remember your alarm list and settings
  • Update the home-screen clock widget and dynamic clock shortcut

This information is not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share your App data with third parties. Because the App does not transmit alarms, settings, sounds, or sensor readings off the device, there is no server-side copy held by the developer.

Google Play and the Android operating system may process limited technical information (for example, install and update metadata) under their own privacy policies when you download or update the App from Google Play. That processing is controlled by Google, not by this App.

5. Permissions

The App requests permissions so alarms can fire and missions can run:

  • Exact alarms — ring at the time you set
  • Notifications — show the alarm alert
  • Vibrate — optional haptic feedback while ringing
  • Run at startup — reschedule alarms after reboot, and when the clock or time zone changes
  • Foreground audio playback — keep the alarm sound playing while it rings
  • Full-screen alerts and wake lock — turn the screen on and show the alarm over the lock screen
  • Physical activity — count steps only during Walk it off
  • Audio files — play a ringtone you pick, or copy a voice clip you choose into the App’s private storage
  • Motion sensors — detect shakes during Shake to stop

The App does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions. A step counter and an accelerometer are used when the device has them; the App can still be installed without those sensors.

6. Data retention and deletion

Alarms and settings stay on your device until you delete an alarm in the App or clear the App’s data. Uninstalling the App removes the local database, settings, and any voice clips stored in the App’s private files, subject to any device backup you have enabled.

Android backup is enabled. Cloud backup includes the alarms database. A full device backup may also include App settings. Those backups are governed by the relevant Google or device-maker privacy policy.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a local alarm clock and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not require an account or online features. If you believe a child has provided personal information through some other channel related to this App, contact us using the details below and we will address the request.

8. Security

Because data stays on your device, security depends on your device’s own protections (screen lock, OS updates, and who has physical access). We do not operate a backend that stores your App data. Voice clips are kept in the App’s private storage, which other apps cannot read.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy if the App’s data practices change (for example, if online features are added later). The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised, and the new policy will be posted at this URL.
