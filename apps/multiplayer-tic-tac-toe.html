Multiplayer Tic Tac Toe

Classic tic-tac-toe stretched to five players on one device — solo vs bot, pass-and-play matches, and boards that grow with the crowd.

Get on Google Play All apps

Gameplay

Pick how many people are playing (1–5). Seats get shapes in a fixed order — circle, cross, triangle, square, pentagon — each with its own colour. Lines count horizontally, vertically, and on both diagonals; a full board with no line is a draw.

Players Board Connect to win
1 (you + bot) 3 × 3 3
2 3 × 3 3
3–4 5 × 5 4
5 6 × 6 4

Features

  • Solo mode with Easy, Medium, and Hard bot difficulty
  • Sound, ambient music, and haptic feedback — mute anytime
  • Resume an unfinished match after closing the app
  • Statistics, streaks, and a leaderboard you can reset
  • Light, dark, and Material You themes
  • Animated draws, winning-line highlight, and confetti on wins
