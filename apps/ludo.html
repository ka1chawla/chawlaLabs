Ludo

Classic board. Four colours. Race your tokens home in a fresh Chawla Labs take on the family favourite.

Ask about early access All apps

How you play

Choose 1–4 players. Solo seats you as Red against a Yellow bot on the opposite corner. Two to four players is pass-and-play — only seated colours take part.

Tap your corner dice to roll, move highlighted tokens, capture rivals, and finish all four pieces to win. Extra turns and captures keep every match lively.

What’s included

  • Red, Green, Blue, and Yellow seats
  • Solo vs bot or local pass-and-play
  • Animated dice and token movement
  • Capture feedback and finish tracking
  • Clear turn prompts — including when the bot is thinking
