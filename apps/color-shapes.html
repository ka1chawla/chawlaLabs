Color & Shapes

Bright puzzles for little learners — match colors, sort shapes, and build early skills through gentle, playful levels.

Ask about early access All apps

Games inside

  • Color Match — tap all balls matching the target color
  • Shape Match — find circles, triangles, and squares
  • Number Order — tap 1…n in order
  • Letter Order — tap letters in ABC order
  • Big & Small Letters — sort uppercase vs lowercase
  • Letters & Numbers — separate digits from letters

Designed for kids

Each game has 10 levels. Item count and complexity rise as they progress, with correct, wrong, win, and tap sounds to keep feedback clear and encouraging.
