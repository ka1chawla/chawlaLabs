8 Ball Pool

World rules. One table. Two ways to play — clear your group, call the pocket, and sink the 8.

Ask about early access All apps Privacy policy

Two ways to play

  • Play the computer — ten levels, from Rookie to Champion, and you choose who breaks
  • Play with a friend — pass-and-play on one phone, with names and a Ready tap so the next aim stays private

On the table

  • Solids and stripes, an open table after the break, and a called pocket for the 8-ball
  • Legal breaks, ball in hand, push outs, and the usual fouls
  • Aim on the felt, set the power, and shoot
  • An aim line you can turn off, plus table sounds and vibration
  • Landscape play, with the rules written into the app
