Smart Alarm

Set a time, pick a mission, and the alarm keeps going until you finish it — on the lock screen, with your sound, even after a reboot.

Ask about early access All apps Privacy policy

Wake-up missions

  • Slide to stop — slide all the way to dismiss
  • Math challenge — solve addition and subtraction
  • Walk it off — walk a target number of steps
  • Shake to stop — shake the phone firmly
  • Tap storm — tap the button until the count is done
  • Color match — tap the matching colors

What you can set

  • Time, label, and repeat days, or a one-time alarm
  • A system ringtone, or a voice clip copied into the app
  • Vibration, a five-minute snooze, and a gradual volume ramp
  • A default mission and volume for new alarms
  • Exact alarms that reschedule after reboot, time, or time-zone changes

On the home screen

Add the Smart Alarm clock widget for a live time, or long-press the app icon for a dynamic clock shortcut that updates every minute.
